# Competitor Playbook: Verizon 5G Home, Verizon Fios, Frontier Fiber, Google Fiber

## Verizon 5G Home (12% of competitor mentions)
- **Their offer:** $25–35/month — but ONLY if the customer already has qualifying Verizon mobile ($65–90/line). True combined cost is $90–115/month.
- **Key weakness:** The cheap price is a mobile-bundle teaser. A customer without Verizon mobile pays far more, and the FWA reliability issues (congestion, weather) match T-Mobile's.
- **Best counter-offer:** TelcoABC One Bundle $49.99 (68% save rate).
- **Talk track:** "That $25 price requires a Verizon phone plan at $65–90 a line. Let's do the real math on your total bill — most customers are looking at $90 to $115 all-in. My TelcoABC One bundle is $49.99 with a mobile line included."

## Verizon Fios (7% of competitor mentions)
- **Their offer:** $50–90/month fiber, East Coast only.
- **Key weakness:** Regional availability, higher price tiers, no integrated live-TV package.
- **Best counter-offer:** TelcoABC One Bundle / Triple Play total-value comparison.
- **Talk track:** "Fios is solid fiber, but it doesn't include integrated TV, and the plan you're comparing runs higher than what I can do. Let me show you the total value of TelcoABC One versus Fios plus a separate TV service."

## Frontier Fiber (11% of competitor mentions)
- **Their offer:** $45–75/month fiber, limited footprint.
- **Key weakness:** Spotty availability, install delays, weaker TV and mobile bundle story.
- **Best counter-offer:** Free Upgrade to Ultra (48% save rate) — meets the speed comparison head-on.
- **Talk track:** "Frontier's fiber is fine where it's available, but install timelines can run weeks and there's no real mobile bundle. I can upgrade your speed to Ultra for free today — no install wait, no service gap."

## Google Fiber (8% of competitor mentions)
- **Their offer:** $70–100/month, available in ~20 metros, no TV or mobile.
- **Key weakness:** Premium price, metro-only, standalone product with no bundle.
- **Best counter-offer:** TelcoABC One Bundle $49.99 (68% save rate).
- **Talk track:** "Google Fiber is fast, but at $70–100 it's a premium standalone product — no TV, no mobile. My TelcoABC One bundle is $49.99 with a mobile line, so you're getting more for meaningfully less."

## General principle for all fiber/5G competitors
Establish the competitor's specific weakness FIRST (reliability, promo cliff, hidden combined cost, or no bundle), THEN present the counter-offer. Never price-match cold — always frame the value gap before the number.
