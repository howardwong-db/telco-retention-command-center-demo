# Retention Offer Catalog

Seven approved retention offers, with historical save rates, cost to TelcoABC, and the customer profile each is best suited for. Match the offer to the customer's situation and the competitor they cite.

## 1. TelcoABC One Bundle — $49.99/month
- **Save rate:** 68% (highest)
- **Cost:** $15/month
- **Best for:** Internet-only customers citing competitor pricing (especially T-Mobile Home Internet, Verizon 5G Home, Google Fiber).
- **Why it works:** Matches competitor price points while adding a mobile line and reliable wired internet. The flagship counter-offer.

## 2. Mobile Line Free for 12 Months
- **Save rate:** 61%
- **Cost:** $29.99/month
- **Best for:** Customers not yet on TelcoABC Mobile.
- **Why it works:** Introduces the bundle relationship; converged customers churn far less.

## 3. 12-Month Price Lock
- **Save rate:** 52%
- **Cost:** $8/month (deferred)
- **Best for:** Customers worried about rate increases or citing a competitor promo (especially AT&T Fiber's expiring intro price).
- **Why it works:** Removes price uncertainty — directly neutralizes promo-cliff competitors.

## 4. Free Upgrade to Ultra
- **Save rate:** 48%
- **Cost:** $12/month
- **Best for:** Speed complainers and customers making fiber speed comparisons (Frontier Fiber, Google Fiber).
- **Why it works:** Meets the speed objection head-on with no install wait.

## 5. Loyalty Credit — $15/month
- **Save rate:** 45%
- **Cost:** $15/month
- **Best for:** Long-tenure customers who feel undervalued.
- **Why it works:** Recognizes loyalty directly; effective on "new customers get better deals" objections.

## 6. $100 Bill Credit
- **Save rate:** 35%
- **Cost:** $100 one-time
- **Best for:** Billing disputes.
- **Why it works:** Resolves the immediate grievance; one-time cost, no ongoing margin impact.

## 7. Free Premium Channels for 6 Months
- **Save rate:** 32%
- **Cost:** Minimal
- **Best for:** TV customers considering cord-cutting.
- **Why it works:** Low-cost value-add that addresses the "cable is too expensive" objection.

## Offer selection principle
Lead with the highest-converting offer the customer's situation supports. For competitive calls, the offer should be the competitor-specific best counter (see competitor playbooks). Do not open with a bill credit or premium-channel giveaway when a bundle or price lock is the better structural fix.
