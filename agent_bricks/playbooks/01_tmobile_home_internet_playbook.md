# Competitor Playbook: T-Mobile Home Internet

## Overview
T-Mobile Home Internet is the #1 competitor cited on TelcoABC retention calls (38% of all competitor mentions). It is a 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) product priced at a flat $50/month with no annual contract. Its aggressive January 2026 promotion is the single largest driver of the current disconnect surge.

## Their Offer
- **Price:** $50/month flat, taxes and fees included, with AutoPay.
- **Technology:** 5G Fixed Wireless Access (cellular signal to a home gateway).
- **Contract:** None. No term commitment.
- **Equipment:** Free gateway included.

## Their Weaknesses (use these)
- **Inconsistent speed:** Real-world speeds range 33–245 Mbps, averaging ~100 Mbps. TelcoABC wired connections deliver consistent, provisioned speeds.
- **Peak-hour congestion:** Because it shares the cellular network, speeds degrade in the evening when the neighborhood is online — exactly when families stream and game.
- **Weather-dependent:** Signal quality drops in heavy rain, snow, and storms.
- **No TV bundle:** Cannot bundle live TV; customer must add a separate streaming service.
- **No wired reliability:** FWA latency is higher and more variable — noticeable on video calls and gaming.

## Best Counter-Offer
**TelcoABC One Bundle at $49.99/month (68% historical save rate).**
Matches T-Mobile on price while adding mobile + reliability. This is the highest-converting play against T-Mobile.

## Recommended Talk Track
1. **Lead with reliability, not price.** "I understand $50 flat sounds great. Can I share what most customers don't realize about 5G home internet before they switch?"
2. **Speed-reliability data:** "T-Mobile advertises 5G, but their published speeds run 33 to 245 Mbps and depend on cell congestion and weather. Your TelcoABC connection is a dedicated wired line — the speed you pay for is the speed you get, every evening."
3. **Then match the price with more value:** "Here's what I can do: our TelcoABC One bundle is $49.99 — that's essentially the same price, but you keep your reliable wired internet AND get a mobile line included."
4. **Close:** "You'd be paying about the same, getting more, and you're not gambling your work-from-home setup on a cell signal."

## Coaching Note
Agents who **lead with speed-reliability data** hit a 68% save rate on T-Mobile calls. Agents who **lead with price-matching alone** hit only 35%. The reliability framing is what converts — always establish the technical weakness before presenting the bundle.
