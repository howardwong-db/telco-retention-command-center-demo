# Competitor Playbook: AT&T Fiber

## Overview
AT&T Fiber is the #2 cited competitor (24% of competitor mentions). The threat spiked when AT&T ran free-fiber-install campaigns across 8 TelcoABC overlap markets in January 2026. It is a genuine fiber product where available, but the promotional pricing and install reality create strong counter-angles.

## Their Offer
- **Price:** $30–80/month depending on speed tier, up to 5 Gbps.
- **Promo:** Introductory pricing with free installation in campaign markets.
- **Contract:** Typically no term, but promo pricing is time-limited.

## Their Weaknesses (use these)
- **Promo expires after 12 months:** Price jumps $25–35/month after the first year. Many customers don't read the fine print.
- **Equipment fees:** Monthly gateway/equipment charges often not included in the headline price.
- **Not always real fiber:** Many advertised addresses actually receive **AT&T Internet Air** (their FWA product), not fiber — same 5G reliability issues as T-Mobile.
- **No mobile bundle:** AT&T does not bundle home internet with mobile the way TelcoABC One does.

## Best Counter-Offer
**12-Month Price Lock (52% historical save rate).**
Directly neutralizes AT&T's biggest weakness — the post-promo price jump — by guaranteeing the customer's rate.

## Recommended Talk Track
1. **Surface the promo cliff:** "That AT&T price is a 12-month promo. Do you know what it goes to in month 13? Most plans jump $25 to $35 a month once the intro period ends."
2. **Check if it's really fiber:** "One thing worth confirming — a lot of addresses in this area get AT&T's Internet Air, which is actually wireless, not fiber. It's worth asking them directly."
3. **Offer certainty:** "Here's what I can do right now — lock your TelcoABC rate for a full 12 months, guaranteed. No surprise jump, no gambling on whether you get real fiber."
4. **Close:** "You get price certainty today, and you keep the reliable service you already know works."

## Coaching Note
The winning move against AT&T is **price certainty**, not speed. Customers tempted by AT&T are price-motivated; the 12-Month Price Lock converts because it removes the risk they didn't know they were taking.
