# Retention Agent Coaching Guide

## The core coaching insight
Across 120,000 retention calls, the single strongest predictor of a save is **how the agent opens the competitor conversation**:
- Agents who **lead with speed-reliability data** (establish the competitor's technical weakness first, then present the offer) achieve a **68% save rate**.
- Agents who **lead with price-matching alone** (jump straight to "I can match that price") achieve only a **35% save rate**.

Coach every agent to establish the value gap before quoting a number.

## Performance tiers (250 agents)
- **Top Performer (67 agents, ~61% save rate):** Consistently lead with reliability/value framing. Use as peer mentors.
- **On Target (122 agents, ~48% save rate):** Solid fundamentals; coach on competitor-specific talk tracks to push toward Top tier.
- **Needs Coaching (53 agents, ~37% save rate):** Often default to price-matching. Priority for talk-track training and call shadowing.
- **At Risk (8 agents, ~27% save rate):** Immediate intervention — pair with a Top Performer, review recent calls, focus on the reliability-first opening.

## Coaching metrics to review per agent
- **Save rate** — primary outcome metric; compare to team average and tier.
- **Offer conversion rate** — are they presenting AND landing the right offer?
- **Average handle time** — very low handle time with low save rate suggests rushing; very high with low save suggests they're not closing.
- **Competitor call rate** — how much of their volume is competitive pressure (context for their save rate).
- **NPS** — customer sentiment even on non-saves.

## Coaching plays by symptom
| Symptom | Likely cause | Coaching action |
|---|---|---|
| Low save, low handle time | Rushing, not discovering the real objection | Slow down; ask why the customer is really calling |
| Low save, high handle time | Talking too much, not closing | Practice the offer close; time-box the objection handling |
| Low save on T-Mobile calls specifically | Price-matching instead of reliability-first | Drill the speed-reliability opening (see T-Mobile playbook) |
| High save but low NPS | Winning the save but damaging the relationship | Coach on empathy and tone, not just outcome |

## What good looks like on a competitive call
1. Acknowledge the competitor offer without dismissing it.
2. Establish the specific weakness (reliability, promo cliff, hidden cost, no bundle).
3. Present the matched counter-offer as "same price / more value."
4. Close with certainty and reassurance.
